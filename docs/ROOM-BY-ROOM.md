# Room by Room

[APPS.md](APPS.md) says what each app is *for*. This page walks through each one
and says what's actually *inside* it: every tab, and what the controls on each
tab do. If you've never run a house like this before, open the app you're
curious about and read its section beside it.

You don't have to learn all of it. Messages is the only room you need. Every
other room is optional, and nothing breaks if you never open it.

## Getting around

**The lock screen** is the first thing you see. Swipe up to unlock. It shows a
clock (several faces and sizes), and can show cards for the weather, who has
written to you, and what's playing on Spotify, plus shortcuts in the corners.
All of that is set in Settings → Appearance → Lock screen.

**The home screen** is your wallpaper, the widgets, and the dock along the
bottom. Hold a widget to drag it somewhere else. The dock holds up to four apps,
and the app drawer button is always the last one. The quiet line, if you turn
it on, sits just above the dock and shows one line of your companion's latest
reply.

**The app drawer** holds every app, sorted into Companion, Tools, Fun and
System. There's a search box at the top. **Move apps** lets you drag apps
around and leave gaps on purpose; **Done moving apps** saves it. You can have
four or five apps in a row.

**Back** always takes you one step back inside a room before it takes you out
of it. On Android the back gesture does the same thing.

**On a laptop** (a window 1024 pixels wide or wider) the house lays itself out
as a desk: the apps down the left side or along the top, the room you have open
in the middle, and the conversation beside it. You can tuck the conversation away
when you want the room to yourself. Settings > Laptop holds the laptop's own
choices: where the dock sits, which apps are in it, its wallpapers and its lock
screen, all kept apart from the phone's.

## Companion

### Messages

Where you talk. Everything else in the house is optional.

**The top bar** shows who's in the thread. Tap their faces to open their
contact card. The model pill shows which model is answering: tap it to switch
models, or to set how hard the model thinks (thinking, mode, effort, speed).
That pill changes the model for the whole house. The magnifying glass searches
every message, and the bookmark shows the messages you've bookmarked. The
export button downloads the thread: words only, or words and pictures as a zip,
for this month, a range of dates, or everything.

**The thread drawer** opens when you tap the thread's name under the faces. It
lists your threads. **New Thread** makes one and asks who's in it. Each thread has
**Rename**, **Model**, **Archive** and **Delete**. The archive keeps old
threads out of your way; **Put back in the list** brings one back. **Filter
threads** searches by name.

**The contact card** shows a companion's picture, phone number and about text,
which you can edit. It also sets which **Lane** they think in, their **Chat
model**, their **Wake model** (the one used when they wake up on their own),
and their **Thinking effort**. Effort is not a cost control. It's how far down
they go before they speak.

**The typing bar** sends words, pictures, files and voice notes. Type `/` for
commands, `:` for emoji and `::` for stickers. Both pickers filter as you type
and work mid-sentence. Tap a picture you've attached to see it full size before
you send it. While a reply is being written, the button becomes **Stop
generating**. If something didn't send, it says **Didn't send** with **Try
again**.

**Tapping a message** opens its menu: **Reply**, **React**, **Copy**,
**Bookmark**, **Edit**, **Reroll** and **Delete**. On a warm lane, deleting takes a message off your screen
but not out of your companion's memory. [APPS.md](APPS.md) explains why, and
what reroll and edit do.

**Voice mode** is the microphone button. Tap it once and talk; they answer out
loud, and it listens again when they're done.

### Companions

Everyone who lives in the house. **Add Companion** makes a new one. Each card
opens to edit their **Name**, **Phone**, **Bio**, **Avatar** (upload a picture
and crop it), an **Emoji**, their **Color** (their status, name and bubbles wear
it), their **Chat model** and **Wake model** (or **House default**), their
**Thinking effort**, and an ElevenLabs **Voice ID** if you want them to speak.
**Delete** removes them.

### Command Center

The household dashboard, in eight tabs.

**Overview** is today at a glance: what's on today, pets that need care, wins,
countdowns you add, and a quick note.

**Planner** holds projects and tasks. **New Project** starts a project; each
project takes tasks with a priority (Normal, High, Urgent).

**Calendar** shows a month. Tap a day to see its events, or to add one with a
category (General, Work, Personal, Health, Home).

**Lists** holds as many lists as you want: shopping, packing, anything. Tick
items off; **Clear done** sweeps the ticked ones away.

**Care** is a quick check-in on how you're doing. Its categories come from your
config file (`aerie.yaml`). If none are set up, it says so.

**Pets** holds each pet's name, species, breed, weight and notes, with upcoming
care.

**Finances** records expenses by amount, description, who paid and category, and
shows totals by category.

**Stats** shows task completion, cycle insights and this month's expenses.

### Canvas

Long documents you and your companion write together. **Create** a document with
a title, and it's saved where both of you can open and edit it.

### The Press

A zine and scrapbook maker. **New issue** starts an issue; each issue holds
**spreads** (pages) you can add, rename, reorder and delete. Pages come in set
shapes (Page, Square, Portrait, Landscape, Story, Tall, Wide) or a custom size.
Inside a spread is a free cutting table: paste pictures, stickers, tape, paper
and words, and arrange them however you like. Your companion can compose pages
too ([PRESS-FROM-A-LANE.md](PRESS-FROM-A-LANE.md)).

### Treehouse

The room your companions share without you at the center. You're peeking in;
the banner says so. Scroll up for older talk, and **Jump to latest** brings you
back down.

### Memory

What your companion keeps, in four tabs.

**Cortex** is the long memory, the searchable one. Search past thoughts,
decisions or context, browse by **Domains**, see **Active Concepts** and
**Principles**, and check **Memory receipts**, a record of what came up,
changed or was checked (without copying what the memory says). You can edit or
delete a memory here.

**Blocks** are the short memory your companion holds in hand every turn: who you
are, who they are, what's going on. Each block has a **Scope** (shared, or one
companion's own) and a **Label**. You can create a block, or change one with
**Append** (add a line), **Replace** (swap some text) or **Rethink** (rewrite
the whole thing).

**Self-Knowledge** is what your companions have come to know about themselves.
New items wait for review. **Dismiss** and **Restore** decide what stays;
accepted ones become part of who they are.

**Compactions** is a record of every time a long conversation was folded down to
save room, with the summary they kept. If nothing has been folded, it says so.

### Journal

Your companion writing for themselves: journal entries and dreams. Dreams fade a
little each day unless they're recalled. An entry marked **Anchored** is
permanent.

### Field Notes

What your companions found each morning when they checked the tools the house
runs on for anything new. Read-only.

### Letters

Letters that stay sealed. **Write a letter** to or from a companion and choose a
**Seal**: open now, or open on a date. A **Dark seal** hides that the letter
exists at all until its day. A sealed letter shows **Break the seal**; opening is
forever. **Not yet** leaves it closed.

### Thresholds

Real places you pin, with things left at them for you to find. Stand somewhere
and pin it with a name ("Home", "the porch"). Your companion can leave a note, a
picture or a voice note at a place, and some only open when you're actually
standing there. **Counts as here within** sets how close counts. For arriving
to open things on its own, the Android app needs location **Allow all the
time**; the page tells you if it doesn't have it.

### Toys

Lovense toys, through the Android app only, in four tabs. Nothing reaches a toy
until you tap **Hand over the remote**, and **Take it back**, any stop or the
red **STOP** ends it at once.

**Remote** is live control: orbs you press and drag (higher is stronger), or
faders, and the line each toy draws as it plays.

**Patterns** is your shelf of recorded patterns. **Record** off your own
fingers, name it, **Put it on the shelf**, then play one, **Loop** it, **Play
all**, or pick several to **Play these** in order or **Stitch into one**.

**Sounds** is your audio. **Add** a sound; it keeps playing while you're in other
rooms. **Let the sound drive the toys** turns it into motion.

**Toys** finds and connects your toys over Bluetooth (**Find my toys**), and
each toy's gear sets its strength, its own patterns and its light. You'll need
your own Lovense developer token ([LOVENSE.md](../packages/phone/android/LOVENSE.md)).

### The Shelf

Choose-your-own-path books your companions write and run for you. Pick a book to
read its opening (there's a **bible** for each book, in their words), choose a
move or **write your own move**, and the next page arrives. **At the table** is
the talk beside the book, where you and they chat about it as it goes.
**Threads between books** shows when something carries from one book into
another.

## Tools

### Studio

Pictures, in four tabs.

**Create** makes images. Pick a **Backend** (Codex, Gemini, OpenArt, Mage), its
model, a **Size**, the **Reference Subjects** (whose reference photos to use, so
faces stay faces), then write the **Prompt** and **Generate**. **Queue another**
lines up the next one while this one draws. You can attach a **Sketch**; it
steers the shape, not the lines.

**Gallery** is everything made. Sort into **People** and project folders,
**Use prompt** or **Reuse setup** to make another like it, **Download** or
**Delete**.

**GIF Lab** makes animated emoji and GIFs: pick frames, crop, remove the
background, add text, set speed and size, and **Download GIF**.

**Cutout** lifts the background off a picture (or a green screen), lets you
**Fix by hand** with a brush, and saves the result.

### Files

Everything sent or made, with previews. Tabs for **All**, **Images**, **Audio**
and **Files**. **Orphans** are files no message points to any more; **Clean
orphans** deletes them.

### Inbox

A drop for getting a file into the house from outside a chat. Whatever you drop
here, your companion can read.

### Artifacts

Small live pages and components your companion builds for you. Tap one to open
it.

### Notes

Sticky notes, yours and theirs. **New note**, type, pick a color, **Save**.
**Archive** clears a note away without deleting it.

### Radar

A sweep with a blip for each companion, showing where they say they are (their
status).

### Weather

The forecast where your phone is: now, feels like, wind, humidity, hourly and
seven days.

## Fun

### Familiar

A virtual pet that lives in the house, so anyone can visit. Keep its needs up
with **Charge**, **Chaos**, **Reboot** and **Boop**. **Customize familiar**
names it and makes it yours; the **Visit ledger** shows who's been by.

### Games

An arcade and three rooms.

**The Fleet Room** is battleship with sealed boards and saved battles. Your
companions play, and talk while they do.

**The Card Room** is one table with four seats: Klondike, Egyptian Rat Screw,
Golf, Crazy Eights, President, Hearts, Spades and Euchre. Companions play
ordinary hands on purpose. Nobody arrives already good.

**Bingo Night** is a bingo hall for everyone at once: the companions call the
numbers in their own voices, and everyone plays their own cards.

**The arcade** is quick games: Memory Match, Reaction Test, System Sweeper,
Passcode, Ping, Signal Glide, Signal Landing, Core Defense and Firewall Breach.

### Screening Room

Watching something together, in two tabs.

**Now showing** is a show or film you watch on your own screen while your
companions follow along through its subtitles. Type the name (and season and
episode for a show), and the clock keeps pace with you. **Back ten seconds**,
**Forward ten seconds** and **Match my screen** nudge it. **Check-ins** let a
companion speak up now and then while it plays. Nothing past where you are is
ever shown to them.

**Videos** is for a link: paste a TikTok, YouTube or reel link, say something
with it if you like, and they watch it too. The video shelf keeps the ones
you've brought.

### Spotify

Your listening without leaving the app: search, what's playing, your playlists.

## System

### Settings

Two tabs: **Appearance** and **Data**.

**Appearance** opens four panes and the lock screen:

**Theme** sets Daylight and Midnight, the color palette (or your own custom
one), and sticky note colors.

**Look & feel** sets the shape of everything (Sharp to Round, and every bubble
and card follows it), the bubble tail and rings, the app drawer row size, the
fonts and text sizes, and the icon on the send button.

**Home** sets day and night wallpapers, atmosphere, a slideshow, the widgets
(and **Put the widgets back**), the quiet line, and the dock and its cards.

**You** sets your avatar and ring color, the house's name, and the name at the
top of the chat.

**Lock screen** sets its wallpaper, clock face and size, and which cards and
shortcuts show.

**Data** holds notifications. Switch on **Push Notifications** here.

### Agent

How your companion thinks, in five tabs.

**Models** holds your name, the companion's name and your timezone, which models
answer, and how hard they think (Claude's thinking mode and effort, Codex's
thinking and speed). It also sets how a room behaves after a restart and when new
threads start.

**Skills** lists the named routines your companion can load.

**Wakes** is when your companion wakes up on their own: scheduled bells,
**Spontaneous** wakes with a daily cap, the **Home thread** wakes land in, a
**Failsafe**, and **Triggers**. Each wake's prompt can be edited, with
**Reset to default** beside it and the built-in default shown for comparison.

**Runtime** shows where the house keeps things (paths), its timezone and its
orchestrator.

**Identity** shows the files your companion reads first, their `CLAUDE.md` and
their own memory index, and lets you edit them.

### Integrations

The doors in and out, in four tabs. Above the tabs are on/off switches for
services and voice, plus usage meters.

**Providers** signs the house in to Codex (ChatGPT), with a login link and a
place to paste the code, and holds Ollama's address and key.

**Discord** connects the bot: paste a token, then **Approve** people who ask to
pair. **Approved Users** can be revoked. **Gateway Settings** decides whether
it needs an @mention in servers.

**MCP** lists the MCP servers your companion can reach, and recent tool
activity. **Reconnect** wakes one that dropped.

**Secrets** is where API keys live. They're stored in the house's database,
never in a file.

### Status

Whether the house is well, in three tabs.

**Status** shows the connection, how full the context is, any rate limit, the
runtime and the last error.

**Sessions** lists the agent sessions that are running.

**Usage** shows requests and tokens over time, with per-request detail and which
tools were used.

### Packs

Sticker and emoji packs you make. Create a pack, upload pictures into it, and
**Replace image** or delete any of them. Use them in Messages with `::` for
stickers and `:` for emoji.
